Welcome

Hello, I’m Sheny Lin, a quantitative UX researcher with interdisciplinary training in information science, communication technology, and human-computer interaction.

My research examines how people interpret, evaluate, and respond to information in technology-mediated environments. I am particularly interested in experimental design, persuasion, user behavior, human–AI interaction, and computational social science.

I hold master’s degrees in Information Science from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and Communication Technology from National Chiao Tung University.

Across academic and applied settings, I have worked on research involving advertising, collaborative technologies, healthcare, and digital products.
